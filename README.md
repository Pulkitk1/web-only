# Consisto – Habit Tracker

**Live website: [consisto-web.vercel.app](https://consisto-web.vercel.app)**

Consisto helps you build better routines by tracking your habits one day at a time. Add the habits you want to keep, tick them off each day, and watch your streaks grow. You earn coins as you stay consistent, and you can use a "freeze" to save your streak when you miss a day.

Most habit trackers make you feel like you failed after one missed day. Consisto is built for flexible habit building, so you can plan rest days, set weekly goals that fit your life, and keep going at your own pace.

You can try it right away as a guest, or sign in to keep your habits safe in your account.

## Features

- **Habits:** create, edit, delete and archive habits.
- **Daily check-ins:** tick off your habits on a weekly view. It shows 5 days on phones and tablets, and the last 7 days on desktop.
- **Flexible weekly goals:** choose how many days a week you want to do a habit.
- **Planned rest days:** schedule days off so they do not count against you.
- **Streaks:** see your current streak and your best streak.
- **Streak freeze:** protect your streak when you miss a day.
- **Coins and wallet:** earn coins as you stay consistent and track them in your wallet.
- **Daily and milestone rewards:** get rewarded for daily progress and for reaching milestones.
- **Today's Progress:** see how much you have completed today at a glance.
- **Calendar view:** see your progress for each habit by month.
- **Notes:** add a note to a completed or missed day.
- **Light and dark themes.**
- **Guest mode:** start with a clean slate and no account needed. Your data stays in your browser.
- **Accounts:** sign in with email and password, reset your password by email, and delete your account from settings.
- **Cloud sync:** when you sign in, your habits stay in sync across devices.
- **Works on any screen:** a layout made for phones, tablets and desktops.

> Reminders are not available on the web yet.

## Technologies Used

- **React Native Web** and **Expo** (with **Expo Router** for navigation)
- **TypeScript**
- **Firebase Authentication** for sign-in
- **Cloud Firestore** for storing account data
- **Vitest** for tests
- **Vercel** for hosting
